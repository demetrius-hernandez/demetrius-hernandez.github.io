Enduring Advantage

Architecture Beats Artifacts

This experience explores bidirectional alignment — designing AI systems that don’t just serve human goals, but actively preserve the human cognitive capacity that enduring advantage requires.

The innovation/counter-innovation cycle is unbreakable if your advantage depends on a specific capability. Drones get countered by jammers. Each side gains weeks of advantage before the other adapts.

The way to break the cycle is to make the advantage architectural. Agentic AI accelerates decisions — while deliberately keeping operators sharp. When the adversary counters the AI, the human can still think, adapt, and fight.

This means designing AI with adjustable autonomy that operators control, building in cognitive fitness so the system actively keeps humans sharp, and engineering fallback readiness that assumes technology will eventually fail.

The human mind is critical infrastructure.
